DaysLite Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Developer: dong.xu · Contact: xudongboy@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy applies to the DaysLite Android application (package name com.dayslite.countdown). It explains what information the app handles and how your data is protected.

In short: DaysLite does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

Data We Store

DaysLite stores only the countdown information you create, kept locally on your device:

  • Countdown event title
  • Target date
  • Event color
  • Yearly repeat setting
  • Optional notes you enter

This information is never uploaded to DaysLite servers or shared with any third party.

Data We Do Not Collect

DaysLite does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. Specifically, the app does not gather identifiers, location information, contacts, payment details, usage analytics, or any tracking data.

Permissions

The current version of DaysLite requires no sensitive Android permissions. It does not request account login, location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or notification access.

Third Parties

DaysLite does not share user data with third parties. The app contains no advertisements and no analytics tools.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete individual countdowns inside the app. You can also delete all app data by uninstalling DaysLite or by clearing the app's storage in Android system settings.

Children's Privacy

DaysLite does not collect any personal information, and therefore does not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated, the change will be reflected by a revised effective date at the top of this page.

Contact

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer at xudongboy@gmail.com.
