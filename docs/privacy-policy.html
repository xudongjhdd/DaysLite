DaysLite Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

Developer: dong.xu

Contact: xudongboy@gmail.com

Overview

DaysLite is a lightweight Android countdown app. This Privacy Policy explains how DaysLite handles information in the current version of the app.

DaysLite does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information We Store

DaysLite lets you create countdown events. The following event information may be stored locally on your device:

  • Countdown event title
  • Target date
  • Event color
  • Yearly repeat setting
  • Optional notes that you enter

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to the developer or to any external server by DaysLite.

Information We Do Not Collect

DaysLite does not collect or request:

  • Name, address, phone number, or email address
  • Account login information
  • Location data
  • Contacts
  • Photos, videos, microphone, or camera data
  • Payment information
  • Analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking data

Permissions

The current version of DaysLite does not require sensitive Android permissions. It does not request access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or files.

The current version also does not request notification permission.

Data Sharing

DaysLite does not share user data with third parties. The app does not include ads, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking tools in the current version.

Data Deletion

You can delete countdown events inside the app. You can also delete all locally stored app data by uninstalling DaysLite or clearing the app storage from Android system settings.

Children's Privacy

DaysLite is a general productivity app and is not designed to collect information from children. Since the app does not collect personal data, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to This Policy

If DaysLite changes how it handles user data, this Privacy Policy will be updated. The effective date at the top of this page will show when the latest version took effect.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact the developer at xudongboy@gmail.com.
