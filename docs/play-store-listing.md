## Short Description

## Full Description

DaysLite is a lightweight countdown app for the dates that matter.

Track upcoming trips, birthdays, exams, anniversaries, launches, holidays, and personal goals in one clean place. Add an event, choose a date, and DaysLite shows exactly how many days are left.

Features:

- Create simple countdown events
- See all important dates in a clean list
- Track days left, today, and days passed
- Add optional notes and colors
- Edit or delete countdowns anytime
- No account required
- No ads
- Data stays on your device

DaysLite is designed to be calm, fast, and easy to use. It helps you keep important moments visible without unnecessary clutter.

Your countdown data is stored locally on your device. DaysLite does not require login and does not collect personal data.
