# DaysLite Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

DaysLite is a lightweight countdown app. DaysLite does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

## Information stored on your device

Countdown titles, target dates, event colors, yearly repeat settings, and optional notes are stored only on your device. DaysLite has no account system, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, or external data server.

## Permissions

DaysLite does not request access to location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files. The current version does not request notification permission.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual countdowns inside DaysLite. You can delete all locally stored DaysLite data by uninstalling the app. Android users may also clear the app's storage in Android system settings.

## Children's privacy

DaysLite is a general productivity app and does not knowingly collect personal information from children or anyone else.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated policy and effective date will be published with the app and on the public privacy-policy page.
