## English (U.S.)

### Subtitle

Countdown important days

### Promotional text

Keep trips, birthdays, exams, and anniversaries in view with simple countdowns stored only on your device.

### Description

DaysLite is a focused countdown app for the dates that matter to you.

Create countdowns for birthdays, anniversaries, trips, exams, deadlines, or any important day. Give each event a color, add an optional note, and keep yearly events recurring automatically.

DaysLite is designed to stay simple:

- See upcoming and past events at a glance
- Create, edit, and delete countdowns quickly
- Repeat birthdays and anniversaries every year
- Choose from six event colors
- Switch between English and Simplified Chinese
- Keep everything locally on your device

No account is required. DaysLite contains no ads or analytics and does not collect your data.
