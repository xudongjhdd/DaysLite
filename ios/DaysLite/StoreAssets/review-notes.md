## English review note

DaysLite is an offline countdown application. It requires no account, login, subscription, purchase, network service, or special hardware.

All functionality is available immediately after launch:

1. Tap the plus button to create a countdown.
2. Enter a title, choose a date and color, optionally add a note or enable yearly repetition, then tap Save.
3. Tap an existing countdown to edit it. The delete action is available at the bottom of the editor and requires confirmation.
4. Open Settings to switch between English and Simplified Chinese or read the bundled privacy policy.

Countdown data and the selected language are stored only in the app's local container on the device. The app has no account system, ads, analytics, tracking, data collection, backend, or third-party SDKs.
