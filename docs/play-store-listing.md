# DaysLite Play Store Listing

## App Title

DaysLite

## Short Description

A simple countdown app for important days.

## Full Description

DaysLite is a lightweight countdown app for the dates that matter.

Use DaysLite to track trips, birthdays, exams, anniversaries, launches, holidays, personal goals, and other meaningful days. Add an event, choose a target date, and see how many days are left at a glance.

DaysLite keeps the experience simple: no account, no ads, and no unnecessary setup. Your countdowns stay on your device, so you can focus on the moments you want to remember.

Features:

- Create countdown events quickly
- Track days left, today, and days passed
- Add optional notes
- Choose colors for different events
- Set yearly repeating countdowns for birthdays, anniversaries, and holidays
- Switch between English and Chinese
- Edit or delete countdowns anytime
- No account required
- No ads
- Countdown data is stored locally on your device

DaysLite is designed to be calm, fast, and easy to use. It helps you keep important dates visible without extra clutter.
