# DaysLite Closed Test Invitation

Thank you for helping test DaysLite, a lightweight Android countdown app.

## Important

Google Play requires testers to remain opted in for at least 14 continuous days. Please do not leave the testing program during this period.

## Join

1. Open the Google Play opt-in link using the Google Account that was invited.
2. Select **Become a tester**.
3. Install DaysLite from the Play Store link shown on the page.
4. Keep the app installed and remain opted in for at least 14 days.
